# About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Electronic Information at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Fudan University.
My research interests lie in LLMs, with a particular focus on pre-training data curation, instruction data synthesis, agent trace data curation, knowledge management, and the cognitive capabilities of language models.
Recently, I have been focusing on the following questions:

+ **Intelligent Agent Workflow**: How can intelligent agents plan, execute, and adapt to complex tasks across different domains and environments? I am particularly interested in task planning, analogical reasoning, structured memory, and compositional workflow synthesis. My research explores how agents can leverage past experiences, identify transferable task structures, and construct new workflows for unfamiliar tasks, rather than relying solely on direct trajectory retrieval.  
+ **Agent Data-Centric Curation**: How can we curate high-quality agent interaction data to improve the learning and generalization capabilities of intelligent agents? I focus on the collection, selection, organization, and synthesis of agent trajectories and tool-use traces. In particular, I am interested in identifying valuable interaction patterns, preserving structural and contextual information, and transforming fragmented execution experiences into reusable training data.
